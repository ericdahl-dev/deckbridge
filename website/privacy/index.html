Privacy

DeckBridge collects no data

The app has no account, no tracking, no analytics and no ads. This website counts visits without cookies.

What DeckBridge collects

Nothing. DeckBridge doesn’t collect, store or send any personal information or usage data, and it contains no analytics or tracking code. Nothing leaves your Mac except the OSC messages and the display feed described below.

Network use

DeckBridge sends OSC messages to the Syndicate DJ app and listens for its feedback. By default that’s on this Mac only (127.0.0.1). If you set it up for Syndicate on another Mac, it uses your local network, and macOS asks for your permission first. If you turn on the display feed, it also sends the deck in front’s state, including track title and artist, unencrypted to the one address you set on your local network. It talks to no other server.

Controller captures

If you use Help add it… or Controller not found?, DeckBridge records the MIDI your controller sends into a file on your Mac. It’s never sent on its own: it reaches the developer only if you email it yourself, from your own mail app. The file holds the controller’s name and ports, the MIDI it sent, the app version and any note you type. It holds no name, email, location or time of day. The web capture works the same way in Chrome, Edge or Firefox: the file is saved on your computer and nothing is uploaded.

What stays on your Mac

Your settings (deck, devices, ports and colors) and any mapping file you import are kept on your Mac, in the app’s own sandboxed storage. Deleting the app removes them.

This website

This website, getdeckbridge.app, counts visits with PostHog so the developer can see which pages are read and where visitors come from. The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser or on your device. To tell visits apart, PostHog turns your IP address and browser details into an anonymous hash that changes every day, and does not keep the address or the details. The same person on two different days therefore looks like two visitors, and visits are not linked to the app. It records the page you open, the page or site that linked you here, and your browser and device type. It also counts clicks on a few links (Join the TestFlight beta, the email address, Read the setup guide and the App Store) and notes which page they were on. On the capture page it counts only that a capture started and finished, never your controller’s name or its MIDI. It does not record your screen, what you type or any other clicks. The fonts are the site’s own. To have your visits removed, email deckbridge@ericdahl.dev.
