Why DeckBridge

Syndicate’s own MIDI mapping (0.3.7) can’t read the LC6000’s 14‑bit jog wheel or pitch fader, and it lights only the DDJ-FLX4 and FLX10. DeckBridge sends both at full resolution and lights the LC6000 from Syndicate’s feedback.

From the LC6000 to Syndicate over OSC

DeckBridge reads the controller’s USB MIDI, sends it to Syndicate’s OSC input on port 9090 and lights the LC6000 from Syndicate’s feedback on port 9096.

Demo mode runs DeckBridge with no hardware

The demo controller plays a script against a simulated Syndicate, so you can watch DeckBridge work before you plug in.

  • Deck layers. Shift + deck select drives Syndicate decks 1 to 4.
  • LEDs. Play, Cue, Sync and the pads follow Syndicate, hot cues in its cue colors.
  • Jog ring. Lit in the loaded deck’s color.
  • Recovery. Unplug or quit and held controls let go; plug back in and the LEDs repaint.

Every control, and what it does in Syndicate

Each control drives the deck of the active layer. Shift changes a few, as noted.

Pitch fader
Tempo at full 14-bit resolution, with pickup so the tempo never jumps
Jog wheel, playing
Phase bend, released 80 ms after the wheel stops
VINYL
Switches the playing jog between bending (off, the default) and seeking (on), for each deck layer; its light is full while seeking
Jog wheel, paused
Seeks through the track
Needle Drop strip
Moves the deck’s channel fader. With Shift, seeks while you touch it.
Play, Cue, Sync, Master
Play/pause, cue (hold), tempo sync, tempo master. PLAY while CUE is held keeps the deck playing; Shift + CUE goes back to the cue point and stops
Shift + Sync (OFF)
Turns sync off on a synced deck; it never turns sync on
Pads: Hot Cue mode
Hot cues 1 to 8, press and hold
Shift + pad, Hot Cue mode
On a stopped deck, goes to that cue without playing, at once. The first press on each cue blips once while DeckBridge learns where it is. For the jump it turns the deck’s quantize off in Syndicate, then sets it to Use Global
Pads: Roll mode
Slip loops from 1/32 to 4 beats, while held
Pads: Loop mode
Saved cue loops
Loop In, Loop Out, Auto Loop
Loop in, loop out, loop; turning the Auto Loop knob halves or doubles it, and Shift + turn moves the loop by its own length
Beat Jump ◀ ▶
Skip a beat back or forward; with Shift, a bar
Censor
Reverse while held; Shift + Censor latches it
Key Lock
Pitch preserve; Shift + Key Lock resets the tempo to 0
Deck select (printed Slip)
Steps to the next deck layer; Shift + deck select steps the unit’s own layer, and DeckBridge follows
Pitch Bend − +
Nudges the tempo while held
Shift + Pitch Bend − +
Transposes the deck down or up a semitone, turning its key lock on first, as Syndicate needs
Param ◀ ▶
Skip a beat back or forward
Browse knob, Back, Forward, Track ◀ ▶
Moves through the library; press to load (Shift + press: Enter)
Shift + browse knob (ZOOM)
Zooms the deck in front’s waveform, one step per click

Questions about DeckBridge

What DeckBridge needs

  • A Mac on macOS 26 or later. DeckBridge lives in the menu bar and has no Dock icon.
  • Syndicate, with OSC input turned on. DeckBridge drives Syndicate over OSC.
  • A Denon LC6000 PRIME over USB. It’s the only controller supported today.
What is DeckBridge?

DeckBridge is a native Mac menu-bar app that lets you play Syndicate from a Denon LC6000 PRIME by bridging the controller’s MIDI to Syndicate OSC. It sends the 14-bit jog wheel and pitch fader to Syndicate at full resolution and lights the controller’s LEDs from Syndicate’s OSC feedback.

Which controllers does DeckBridge support?

DeckBridge supports one controller today, the Denon LC6000 PRIME, connected over USB. A single LC6000 drives all four Syndicate decks through its own deck layers. To help add yours, DeckBridge’s Help add it… (in the panel) or Controller not found? (in Settings) records what your controller sends, and you choose whether to email the file. Without the app, record it in your browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox).

What are the requirements for DeckBridge?

DeckBridge needs macOS 26 or later, Syndicate with OSC input enabled, and a Denon LC6000 PRIME connected over USB. It runs in the menu bar with no Dock icon.

How much does DeckBridge cost?

DeckBridge is free. Version 1.1.0 is in beta on TestFlight now, and the Mac App Store version is coming soon.

How does DeckBridge setup work?

To set up DeckBridge, you change four settings in Syndicate once: turn on OSC input (port 9090), point OSC feedback at 127.0.0.1 port 9096, turn on the listed feedback subscriptions, and turn the LC6000’s MIDI input off in Syndicate. DeckBridge opens a setup guide with these steps on first launch, and it shows “Done: feedback live” once Syndicate answers.

Does DeckBridge need djay Pro or Engine DJ?

No, DeckBridge works with Syndicate only and doesn’t need djay Pro or Engine DJ to run the LC6000. Quit those apps or any other app using the LC6000 first, and power-cycle the controller if one was open, because they can switch its MIDI mode until it restarts.

Is DeckBridge made by or affiliated with Untitled Audio Syndicate Ltd. or Denon DJ?

No, DeckBridge is an independent app, not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Untitled Audio Syndicate Ltd. (the makers of Syndicate) or Denon DJ. It works with Syndicate through Syndicate’s OSC API.

Does DeckBridge collect any data?

No, the DeckBridge app collects no data: no account, no tracking, no analytics. It sends OSC messages to Syndicate and listens for its feedback, on this Mac by default or on your local network if you choose that. If you turn on the display feed, it also sends the deck in front’s state, including track title and artist, unencrypted to the one address you set on your local network. This website counts visits with PostHog, without cookies; the privacy page has the details.

Can I try DeckBridge without an LC6000?

Yes, DeckBridge’s demo controller runs a scripted controller against a simulated Syndicate, so you can see it work without an LC6000. Nothing goes to the network unless you turn on the display feed, and then only unencrypted to the one address you set on your local network. Demo mode is off again at the next launch.

Can I use two LC6000s, or run Syndicate on another Mac?

Yes, DeckBridge can drive a second LC6000 on another Syndicate deck, and it can reach Syndicate running on another Mac over your local network. Add the second unit under Settings › Add controller; both share Syndicate’s one feedback stream. For another Mac, set Syndicate’s host to that Mac’s IP address and set feedback to listen on All networks. macOS asks once for local-network permission.

What’s new in DeckBridge 1.1?

DeckBridge 1.1 shows the deck in front on the LC6000’s jog-wheel display: its title, artist, key and BPM, Syndicate’s master clock around the ring, and the deck’s own bar in the needle. Diagnostics › Jog display shows the same picture on your Mac. It also adds Shift + Pitch Bend to transpose, CDJ-style cue, Needle Drop on the channel fader, exact colors on the ring and pads, a key display in traditional or Camelot, and a display feed to a display on your local network, off by default.
