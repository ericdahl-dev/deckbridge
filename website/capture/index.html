Help add a controller

Record your controller for DeckBridge

If your DJ controller isn’t supported yet, this page walks you through pressing, moving and turning its controls, about five minutes, and records the MIDI it sends. The file is saved on your computer and nothing is uploaded; you choose whether to email it to us.

This browser can’t read MIDI

Safari doesn’t support Web MIDI. Open this page in Chrome, Edge or Firefox, or use the DeckBridge app on your Mac: Settings › Controller › Controller not found?

Before you start

  1. Plug in the controller and close other DJ apps that use it.
  2. Press Start. Your browser asks to use MIDI devices; allow it.
  3. Follow each step, then press Next. Skip anything your controller doesn’t have.

Only MIDI from the controller you pick is recorded. DeckBridge never sends anything to it from this page.
